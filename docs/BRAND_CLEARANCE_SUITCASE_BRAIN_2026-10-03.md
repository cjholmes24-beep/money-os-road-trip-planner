# Preliminary Brand Clearance — Suitcase Brain

Date: 2026-10-03
Status: **working brand candidate, not legal clearance**

## Candidate
**Suitcase Brain**

Positioning:
- consumer-facing travel intelligence engine
- powered by Money OS
- broad enough for trip planning, discovery, group logistics, recovery and vendor opportunity
- deliberately more memorable than a generic "AI Trip Planner" name

## Preliminary collision search

Searches performed across general web results, travel/software queries, app-store-indexed results, and trademark-indexed web results found no obvious direct travel-planning software or travel-service brand using the exact phrase **Suitcase Brain**.

Searches did find:
- generic stock/vector artwork described as a "suitcase brain" logo concept
- ordinary phrase uses and unrelated media references
- no obvious exact-match travel-planning product surfaced in the preliminary search

## What this does NOT prove

This is not an attorney trademark opinion and does not prove:
- federal/state/common-law availability
- domain availability or ownership
- social-handle availability
- international trademark availability
- freedom from confusingly similar marks

Before spending money on an LLC name, domain, trademark filing, logo filing or patent work:
1. run a fresh USPTO search for exact and confusingly similar marks in relevant classes
2. search state business registries as needed
3. search common-law commercial use
4. check domain/social/app-store conflicts
5. obtain professional trademark review when the business economics justify the spend

Until then, use **Suitcase Brain** as a working public product/engine name with no claim that registration is guaranteed.
