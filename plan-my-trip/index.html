YOUR SAFETY COMES FIRST

Leave-trip plan

If danger is immediate, contact local emergency services now. This tool does not provide legal-evasion advice.

  1. Move to personal safety.
  2. Find safe lodging if needed.
  3. Arrange transportation home or elsewhere.
  4. Secure identification, medicine, money, phone/charger, keys, and essential belongings.
  5. Review which reservations can still be canceled.
  6. Protect remaining money and reserve.
  7. Confirm who owns each rental, lodging, or transport booking.
  8. Contact trusted people and official resources.
Official resources: use the local emergency number for immediate danger; contact your country's embassy/consulate when abroad; contact the provider using the number or URL on your verified reservation. Use official local resources for current contact details.

METHOD & PLANNING QUESTIONS

Understand the estimate before booking

Suitcase Brain · Planning explanation reviewed October 6, 2026. Calculation inputs are user entered; provider rules and prices must be checked for your trip.

What does the trip budget represent?

It is your entered plan, not live provider pricing. Keep required costs, optional spending and a contingency reserve distinct. Unknown transport, lodging and event components remain unknown in their quote workspaces.

Is the per-person share a promise everyone has paid?

No. Acceptance and funding are separate states. The blueprint explains the current split and what happens when fewer people cover the entered planned total. It does not collect payments or enforce commitments.

How should I compare hotel quotes?

Enter the same dates, rooms and occupancy, then base stay, taxes and mandatory fees. Verify parking and optional costs separately. An incomplete price cannot be called cheapest; different currencies are not ranked without conversion.

Does a missing policy mean no cancellation loss?

No. Enter known refundable and nonrefundable amounts, fees and deadlines from the policy you checked. Incomplete rules are unresolved exposure, not a zero-risk booking. The scenario is planning math, not a provider refund guarantee.

What if a traveler accepts but has not funded a share?

Record the actual commitment/funding state. The unpaid-traveler and dropout views show the burden under their stated assumptions; they do not treat acceptance as cash. Resolve responsibility before making nonrefundable commitments.

Is a reserve included in the spendable budget?

The reserve is deliberately separated from the normal spending plan. Choose an amount appropriate to your situation; the tool does not claim a universal safe percentage or guarantee it covers every emergency.
