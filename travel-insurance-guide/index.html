TRIP PROTECTION

Travel Insurance Guide

Travel insurance is a contract, not a magic shield. Compare what is covered, what is excluded, the limits, and how a claim has to be documented.

Start with what you actually need protected

Common policy categories can include trip cancellation or interruption, emergency medical expenses, emergency evacuation, baggage loss or delay, and certain travel delays. Not every policy contains every category, and definitions, limits, deductibles, and exclusions can differ.

Read the details that change the value

  • Maximum benefit limits for each coverage category.
  • Deductibles, waiting periods, and documentation requirements.
  • Covered reasons for cancellation or interruption.
  • Pre-existing-condition rules and any waiver requirements.
  • Geographic limits, trip-length limits, and activity exclusions.
  • Whether reimbursement is primary or secondary to other coverage.

Compare policies using the same trip facts

Use the same travelers, dates, destination, trip cost, and coverage priorities when comparing policies. A lower premium can come with lower limits or narrower coverage, so price alone does not describe the contract.

Before buying

Read the certificate or policy wording supplied by the insurer. Confirm cancellation terms and know how to reach assistance while traveling. Keep receipts and written records relevant to a possible claim.

METHOD & PLANNING QUESTIONS

Understand the estimate before booking

Suitcase Brain · Planning explanation reviewed October 6, 2026. Calculation inputs are user entered; provider rules and prices must be checked for your trip.

How do I prepare for a policy comparison?

List the actual nonrefundable bookings, dates, travelers and protection priorities. Keep quotes on the same basis. This page is a planning checklist, not an insurance quote, recommendation of suitability or promise a claim will be accepted.

Does the lowest premium mean the best value?

A premium only describes one part of the contract. Compare deductibles, limits, covered reasons, exclusions and assistance terms for your facts. There is no invented typical premium or guaranteed payout here.

Does cancellation cover any reason I change my mind?

Do not assume it. Check the exact covered reasons, documentation, deadlines and any separately offered cancellation option in the policy wording. Plan My Trip can list your entered cancellation exposure, but that is not an insurer coverage determination.

Which exclusions should I read?

Review the actual wording for your trip, including geographic/trip-length limits, activity exclusions, medical-condition rules and claim requirements. These can differ. Resolve uncertain terms with the insurer or a qualified adviser before relying on coverage.
