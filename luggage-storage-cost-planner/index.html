When luggage storage may help

Storage can bridge the time between an early arrival and accommodation check-in, a late departure and checkout, or a long connection. It can also make sightseeing easier when a station or accommodation will not hold bags.

Check more than the price

Confirm opening hours, maximum bag size, prohibited items, identification requirements, access rules, insurance or liability limits, cancellation terms, and the exact location. Some services charge per calendar day rather than each 24-hour period; match this calculator’s unit to the provider’s rule.
