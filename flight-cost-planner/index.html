METHOD & PLANNING QUESTIONS

Understand the estimate before booking

Suitcase Brain · Planning explanation reviewed October 6, 2026. Calculation inputs are user entered; provider rules and prices must be checked for your trip.

What does this flight total include?

The total is travelers × (round-trip airfare + baggage + seat or airline fees), plus the group airport-transport amount. Baggage and seat inputs are per traveler for the full trip. Enter airport access for the group only once. Lodging, activities and destination transport are outside this estimate.

How do I count bags without doubling fees?

Work out the charge for every charged leg and bag from the actual fare rules, then enter one full-trip baggage total per traveler. If travelers have different bags, use a documented group average or calculate separately. A blank optional field is treated as zero in this simple calculator; it does not prove the airline charges nothing.

Should I include seat selection?

Enter a seat charge only if that paid selection is part of your plan. Check whether the quote covers each traveler and each leg. A fare bundle may already include it; avoid entering the same charge twice.

Where does airport parking fit?

Combine parking or departure-airport access with destination transfers in the group transport field. Verify how parking days are charged. Use the airport-transfer planner to turn a one-way group quote into a return-trip estimate, then add any separate parking.

What changes for a family?

Use the actual fares and bag/seat requirements for the people traveling. This tool does not assume children travel free, sit together without a charge, or have an automatic discount. Verify those facts with the carrier and keep child-seat costs in transfer quotes when applicable.

How should a group share the result?

The per-traveler figure is the group total divided equally; it is not a payment commitment. Different fares or unequal allocations need a separate group plan. Plan My Trip separates acceptance, funding and dropout exposure.

How do I compare flying with driving?

Use the same people and round-trip scope. Compare the complete flight total with fuel, tolls, parking and extra road costs. Travel time, fatigue and destination mobility remain separate decisions. The flight-versus-driving comparison below uses entered costs, not live fare or route data.

Illustrative arithmetic, not a live fare: two travelers with $200 airfare, $40 baggage and $10 seat charges each, plus $60 group airport access, total $560: 2 × (200 + 40 + 10) + 60.
