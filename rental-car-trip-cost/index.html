Look beyond the advertised daily rate

A daily rate multiplied by rental days gives only the base rental. Provider taxes, airport surcharges, optional products, young-driver charges, one-way fees, deposits, fuel policy, and toll-service rules can materially change the checkout price. Enter only charges that apply to your quote.

METHOD & PLANNING QUESTIONS

Understand the estimate before booking

Suitcase Brain · Planning explanation reviewed October 6, 2026. Calculation inputs are user entered; provider rules and prices must be checked for your trip.

What does the rental total cover?

The calculation is daily rate × rental days, plus entered taxes/fees, fuel, parking and tolls. A quoted package may already include some of these: do not add an included cost twice. Deposits are cash holds, not automatically spending.

Are taxes included in an advertised rate?

Do not assume either way. Read the final quote and identify the base charge and any payable taxes or fees. Check driver, airport, one-way and optional-product terms that actually apply; there is no universal fee schedule here.

How should I estimate fuel?

Use realistic route mileage, MPG and a fuel price you verified in the gas calculator. Check the rental refueling agreement separately. This field is the fuel total for the rental, not a per-day price.

What parking should I include?

Add hotel, destination and overnight parking that applies to the whole rental. Ask whether the stay quote includes parking before counting it again. Blank fields in this quick tool mean zero included in the arithmetic, not verified free parking.

When should I compare a transfer instead?

A rental provides mobility beyond the airport journey; a transfer may only cover arrival and departure. Compare the same group, luggage and full itinerary. Add planned local rides to the transfer alternative before treating either as a complete ground-transport plan.

Illustrative arithmetic, not a rental quote: 3 days × $40, plus $30 fees, $20 fuel and $15 parking/tolls, equals $185. Verify each applicable component yourself.
