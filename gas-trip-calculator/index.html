How to estimate gas for a trip

The calculator divides total driving miles by miles per gallon, then multiplies the gallons by your entered fuel price. A round trip doubles the one-way distance. Include likely local driving or detours in the mileage if they matter to your plan.

Choose realistic inputs

Highway ratings can differ from loaded, real-world fuel economy. Terrain, traffic, weather, speed, tire pressure, roof boxes, and idling can change consumption. Fuel prices also vary along a route, so use a route average rather than assuming every stop costs the same.
