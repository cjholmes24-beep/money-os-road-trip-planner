Make the two totals comparable

The fuel calculation reuses the existing transportation intelligence formula: total route miles ÷ realistic MPG × your fuel price. It adds only your entered other road costs. The flight figure is a total you calculated or verified, not a fare fetched by this page. All figures here use USD; no currency conversion is connected.

Use the same travelers, return scope and destination for both alternatives. If both need the same hotel nights and meals, compare consistently; if driving needs an extra overnight stay or flying needs local transport, include that difference. Vehicle wear, time away from work, accessibility and fatigue are not silently assigned a dollar amount.

When does group size change the decision?

A ticket is often priced per traveler while a car journey can share some costs. That does not prove driving is cheaper: vehicle capacity, luggage, rentals, extra rooms and different fares matter. Recalculate from actual quotes if the group changes; do not compare one airline ticket with a full-car trip.

Illustrative example, not a fare or fuel quote: a two-person flight plan totals $560. A 600-mile road journey at 30 MPG and an entered $4 per gallon uses 20 gallons, or $80 fuel. With $120 other known road costs, the scoped road total is $200. The $360 arithmetic difference is not guaranteed savings: unresolved rental, overnight or destination transport costs may change it.

Questions to resolve before booking

Should I compare driving fuel with airfare?

Compare complete same-scope totals instead. Add known baggage, airport access and destination transport to the flight plan; add road-only lodging, parking, tolls and rental charges to the drive plan.

Does a smaller entered total mean the best option?

No. It only describes the costs entered. Check required unknown components and compare travel duration, rest, accessibility, cancellation terms and your ability to arrive when needed.

Where do the numbers come from?

From your inputs and the existing fuel formula. This page retrieves no live fares, routing, station prices or provider availability. Suitcase Brain planning explanation reviewed October 6, 2026.
