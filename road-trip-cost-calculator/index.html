Build a complete road trip budget

Fuel is only one part of driving costs. Enter lodging as a trip total so you can include room taxes or multiple rooms. Food is calculated per traveler per day. Tolls, parking, and miscellaneous costs remain separate so overlooked expenses are easier to spot.

What is not included automatically?

Vehicle wear, insurance, repairs, deposits, exchange-rate changes, and attraction tickets are not inferred. Add relevant amounts to miscellaneous or use the activities planner. The per-person figure assumes every entered cost is shared evenly; change it manually if your group splits costs differently.
