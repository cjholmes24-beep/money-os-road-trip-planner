PLAN BETTER

Use estimates as a buffer, not a promise

Real trips rarely match a perfect spreadsheet. Construction, detours, weather, idling, traffic, and extra local driving can increase fuel use. A small mileage buffer can make the estimate more realistic.

Your real-world MPG can differ because of speed, cargo weight, terrain, wind, tire pressure, and climate control. If you are unsure, use a slightly lower MPG number so the budget is less optimistic.

Lodging totals can change because of taxes, parking charges, resort fees, or refundable deposits. Check the full price shown by the hotel before making a reservation.

FORMULAS

What the calculator is doing

Fuel: trip miles ÷ MPG × fuel price.

Hotels: nightly rate × hotel nights.

Food: daily food budget × travelers × travel days.

Total: fuel + hotels + food + miscellaneous costs.

Per person: total trip cost ÷ travelers.

These are planning estimates only. Verify current prices before spending money.
