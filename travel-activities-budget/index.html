Plan paid and free time together

The estimate multiplies travelers, attractions per day, average per-person cost, and travel days. An average allows a museum on one day and a free park on another without listing every stop. Decimal attractions are allowed: 1.5 per day means three paid activities across two days.

Check booking fees, taxes, equipment rental, transport, tips, and age-based pricing. Leave open time rather than booking every hour, and confirm cancellation and weather policies for time-sensitive activities.
