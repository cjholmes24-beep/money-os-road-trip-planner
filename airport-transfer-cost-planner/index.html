Compare airport transport on the same basis

Taxi: may offer flexible pickup, but meter rules, traffic, queues, luggage charges, and airport supplements can affect cost. Private transfer: may provide a reserved pickup and stated vehicle size; confirm waiting time and cancellation terms. Public transport: can cost less, but consider operating hours, transfers, accessibility, luggage, and the walk to your accommodation.

Compare the total group price, not just a per-person headline. Confirm whether a round-trip quote includes both legs and whether child seats, oversized bags, waiting, tips, or late-night service cost extra.
