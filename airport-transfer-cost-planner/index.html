METHOD & PLANNING QUESTIONS

Understand the estimate before booking

Suitcase Brain · Planning explanation reviewed October 6, 2026. Calculation inputs are user entered; provider rules and prices must be checked for your trip.

What price belongs in this field?

Enter the quoted one-way total for your whole group, not a single-person ticket. The tool multiplies that price by one or two journeys and divides it by travelers. Different return pricing requires a separate calculation or a documented average.

How do I use a per-person shuttle fare?

First multiply the actual single-person fare by the travelers who must pay, including any required bag or booking charges. Enter that group one-way amount. Do not multiply a price already quoted for the whole vehicle again.

What can a transfer quote leave out?

Check waiting-time allowances, flight-delay handling, late-night charges, pickup instructions, child seats and cancellation. The planner does not fetch operating hours or confirm availability. A missing charge is unknown until you verify it.

What must a group verify before reserving?

Confirm vehicle capacity for both people and luggage, accessibility and any required child seats. The per-person result assumes an equal split. It does not promise one vehicle can serve the group.

Illustrative arithmetic, not a shuttle quote: a $60 one-way group quote used twice equals $120. Four travelers sharing equally would plan $30 each.
